Stop wrestling with bloated robotics frameworks. Start building.

PeppyOS cuts through the complexity so you can focus on what matters: bringing your robots to life. Whether you're prototyping your first project or scaling production systems, PeppyOS gives you a streamlined stack that just works.

This guide will walk you through the installation of PeppyOS on your system and creating a few nodes to get a feel of how the whole system works
