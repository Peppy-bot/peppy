v0.31.1 https://docs.peppy.bot/releases/v0-31-1/ 2026-09-24T00:00:00Z On Linux, container builds authenticate to private registries with your Docker or Apptainer login. <article> <header> <h1>v0.31.1</h1> <p><em>On Linux, container builds authenticate to private registries with your Docker or Apptainer login.</em></p> <p><small> Released on September 24, 2026 </small></p> </header> <ul> <li>On Linux, pull base images from a private registry by logging in with <code>docker login</code> or <code>apptainer registry login</code> before you build; peppy hands your credentials to Apptainer.</li> <li>Builds that used Docker's browser login no longer fail at random with "incorrect username or password", because peppy leaves Docker's session tokens out of the credentials it gives Apptainer.</li> <li>Log in or out while the daemon runs and the change applies to your next build, with no daemon restart.</li> </ul> </article>
