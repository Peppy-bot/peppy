v0.22.0 https://docs.peppy.bot/releases/v0-22-0/ 2026-08-05T00:00:00Z Slots gain cardinality-based sizing, links can be left vacant, and refreshes report unreadable repositories instead of failing. <article> <header> <h1>v0.22.0</h1> <p><em>Slots gain cardinality-based sizing, links can be left vacant, and refreshes report unreadable repositories instead of failing.</em></p> <p><small> Released on August 5, 2026 </small></p> </header> <h3>Breaking changes</h3> <ul> <li><code>defer_links</code> is replaced by a vacant link value. Replace any <code>defer_links</code> entry in your links maps with the vacant link value.</li> <li>Participant slots no longer accept the <code>optional</code> field. Express whether a participant slot may be left unfilled through its cardinality instead.</li> </ul> <h3>Slots and links</h3> <ul> <li>Producer slots now bind at most one producer.</li> <li>Observer slots are sized by their cardinality.</li> <li>A link may be left vacant only when the node manifest declares itself optional.</li> </ul> <h3>Repository refresh</h3> <ul> <li>A refresh now reports the repositories it could not read and continues instead of failing the entire run.</li> </ul> </article>
