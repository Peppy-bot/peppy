v0.25.5 https://docs.peppy.bot/releases/v0-25-5/ 2026-08-21T00:00:00Z Pin a contract or pairing's generic arrays to your node's length with `refine`, and harden how servers decode goals. <article> <header> <h1>v0.25.5</h1> <p><em>Pin a contract or pairing's generic arrays to your node's length with `refine`, and harden how servers decode goals.</em></p> <p><small> Released on August 21, 2026 </small></p> </header> <ul> <li>Pin a contract or pairing document's generic arrays to a fixed length for your node with a <code>refine</code> block on an implements, consumes, or pairing entry.</li> <li>A generated action server now rejects a goal whose request bytes do not fit its declared shape with the decode error and keeps serving, never reaching your decider.</li> <li>Python nodes now raise a <code>ValueError</code> when a fixed-length array is encoded or decoded with any other number of elements, matching Rust's fixed-size arrays.</li> </ul> </article>
